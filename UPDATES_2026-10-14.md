## Research & Market Articles Image Fix (`/` Homepage)
- **Root Cause**: The articles section had fallback paths (`/counterpoint_1.png`, `/counterpoint_2.png`, `/idc.png`, `/eco.png`) and external API links where images were missing in `frontend/public`, causing broken image icons with alt text.
- **Solution & Assets Generated**:
  1. Generated 4 high-resolution, custom infographic editorial report images:
     - `counterpoint_1.png`: "India Smartphone Market Growth - Trends & 5G Growth Curve"
     - `counterpoint_2.png`: "The Rise of the Refurbished Smartphone - Diagnostic Pipeline"
     - `idc.png`: "Tech Market Intelligence - Second-Hand Devices Outpacing New Devices"
     - `eco.png`: "Global Research - The Sustainable Electronics Economy & E-Waste Recycling"
  2. Placed the images directly in `frontend/public/` for instant, reliable local rendering.
  3. Added an automatic `onError` fallback handler in `market-opportunity.tsx` to prevent broken image placeholders under any network/API circumstances.
