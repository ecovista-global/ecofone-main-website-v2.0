## 📌 Summary of Changes

### 1. Setu-Style Modern Footer Architecture
- Completely redesigned the website footer to match the clean, horizontal, left-aligned design of the Setu model while maintaining EcoFone's dark forest green (`#071f12`) brand aesthetic.
- **Brand Identity Badge**: White rounded pill containing the EcoFone logo, vertical divider, and `FRANCHISE NETWORK` uppercase label.
- **Circular Social Chips**: Minimalist translucent circular buttons for LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, 𝕏, and Facebook.
- **Corporate Meta Block**: Left-aligned, clean typography displaying the company tagline, legal entity (**ECOVISTA GLOBAL PRIVATE LIMITED**), Registered HQ address, and direct corporate contact channels.
- **Horizontal Navigation Row**: Clean, spacious text link strip featuring `Home`, `About Us`, `Franchise Program`, `Careers`, `Articles / Blogs`, and `Contact Us`.
- **Bottom Legal Row**: Subdued divider line with copyright and `Privacy Policy` / `Terms of Use` links.

### 2. Elimination of Unwanted Public Elements
- **Removed "⭐ Write a Customer Review" Button**: Stripped out the consumer review submission popup button from the corporate portal footer.
- **Removed "Verify Certificate" Public Link**: Removed the public footer sample link (`EF-2026-8842`). The verification system remains intact on the backend for QR code scanning on issued certificates, but is removed from public customer links.
- **Removed Google Map Embed**: Excised the heavy map iframe from the footer.

### 3. Complete Purge of `/services` Route & Permanent Redirection
- Deleted `frontend/app/services/` (`page.tsx`) entirely.
- Added a `308 Permanent Redirect` in `frontend/next.config.js` to automatically redirect any incoming requests from `/services` directly to the homepage (`/`).
- Updated internal card links across `revenue-streamdesk.tsx` and `what-makes-different.tsx` to route to `/franchise` and `/about`.
- Cleaned up layout references in `layout-client-wrapper.tsx`.

### 4. Header Navigation Cleanup
- Reverted the top header navbar to 4 core links: `Home`, `About Us`, `Franchise`, and `Contact Us` (with the `Apply for Franchise` CTA button), keeping `Careers` cleanly housed in the footer navigation.
