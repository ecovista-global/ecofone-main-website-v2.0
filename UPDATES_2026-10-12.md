## 6. Rate Limiting Security (`frontend/lib/rate-limit.ts`)

Developed an in-memory sliding-window rate limiter utility to safeguard all public submission endpoints against automated scripts, bots, and repeated form submission abuse:

* **Utility:** `frontend/lib/rate-limit.ts`
* **Mechanism:** Resolves client IP address via `x-forwarded-for`, `x-real-ip`, or `cf-connecting-ip`.
* **Rule:** Maximum **5 submissions per 10 minutes** per IP address.
* **Exceeded Response:** Returns standard **HTTP 429 Too Many Requests** with header `Retry-After: <seconds>` and a friendly cooldown notice.
* **Protected Routes:**
  1. `/api/contact`
  2. `/api/franchise/lead`
  3. `/api/careers/applications`

Created a centralized email builder ([`frontend/lib/email-templates.ts`](file:///d:/ecofone%20main%20website/frontend/lib/email-templates.ts)) to provide a consistent, premium brand experience across all system emails:

### Visual Layout:
- **Header:** Deep forest-green gradient (`#052e16` ➔ `#064e3b` ➔ `#047857`).
- **Logo:** High-resolution `public/logo.png` embedded inline via `cid:ecofonelogo` to bypass email client image blockers.

- **Card Body:** 20px rounded corners, subtle drop-shadow, and light slate background.
- **Mint Highlight Quote:** Soft mint box (`#f2fbf6`) with a 4px solid orange left-border for inquiry text or role references.

### Stream Identity Separation:

| **`business@ecofone.co.in`** | **`EcoFone Business`** | `"EcoFone Business" <business@ecofone.co.in>` | **EcoFone Business Team** | Contact inquiries & Franchise leads (Notifications + Auto-confirmations) |
