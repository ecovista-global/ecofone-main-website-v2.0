## 1. Executive Summary

Today's sprint focused on refactoring and elevating the **EcoFone Blog / TechByte Editorial Platform** (`/blog` and `/blog/[slug]`), unifying authentic brand colors, eliminating visual AI artifacts, and integrating official company social media channels across the entire web application:

- **Cashify-Style Editorial Architecture**: Upgraded the blog listing and reading interfaces to Cashify’s high-density editorial layout while strictly adhering to EcoFone's brand design system.
- **Full-Width Canvas**: Expanded container widths from restrictive boxed layouts to an expansive `1440px` viewport with a structured 68% / 32% two-column split.
- **Anti-AI Design Cleanup**: Replaced pill/egg rounded corners with sharp rectangular framing (`rounded-none` / 0px border-radius) and replaced SVG vectors with high-resolution photorealistic hardware photography.
- **Brand Palette Unification**: Standardized typography, accents, and components around EcoFone's brand identity: Deep Forest Green (`#071f12`), EcoFone Orange (`#ea580c`), and Emerald Eco Green (`#16a34a`).
- **Navigation Streamlining**: Removed duplicate sub-navbars (`TechByteSubnav`) from blog pages to maintain single-source navigation via the global navbar.
- **Sidebar De-cluttering**: Removed the "Top Offers" e-commerce card from sidebar widgets, focusing attention on editorial articles, authors, and reader engagement.
- **Official Social Media Integration**: Connected all official social media channels (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and X) across the footer, blog sidebars, and Schema.org structured data.

## 2. Editorial Layout & Grid Architecture (`/blog` & `/blog/[slug]`)

### Viewport Expansion:
- **Previous State:** Constrained inside narrow `max-w-6xl` containers, creating excessive white margins on wide desktop displays.
- **New Implementation:** Expanded outer wrappers across the blog platform to `w-full max-w-[1440px] mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-10 xl:px-12`.

### Responsive Two-Column Grid:
- **Left Editorial Column (`68%` / `lg:col-span-8`):** Houses Featured Hero Stories, Latest Stories stream, Quick Reads / News Carousels, and long-form article content.
- **Right Sticky Sidebar (`32%` / `lg:col-span-4`):** Features the Social Community Widget, "You May Also Read" curated posts, and "First Impressions" hardware reviews. Sticky positioning (`lg:sticky lg:top-28`) ensures continuous visibility during reading.
