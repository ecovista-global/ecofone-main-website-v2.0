## 1. Executive Summary

In this engineering update, we performed a major design and structural cleanup of the EcoFone web portal to elevate the user experience, remove legacy clutter, and align the site's footer and navigation with modern tech startup standards (specifically inspired by the Setu layout while preserving EcoFone's signature brand identity):

1. **Setu-Inspired Modern Footer Overhaul**:
   - Replaced the legacy 4-column widgetized layout with a clean, full-width, left-aligned tech layout.
   - **Top Brand & Social Strip**: Features a white rounded badge with the EcoFone logo, vertical divider, and `FRANCHISE NETWORK` uppercase badge on the left, paired with translucent circular social media buttons (LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, 𝕏, Facebook) on the right.
   - **Corporate Information Block**: Clean, prominent typography detailing the brand tagline, legal entity (**ECOVISTA GLOBAL PRIVATE LIMITED**), Registered HQ address, and direct corporate contact channels.
   - **Horizontal Navigation Bar**: A single, clean horizontal row displaying core navigation routes (`Home`, `About Us`, `Franchise Program`, `Careers`, `Articles / Blogs`, `Contact Us`).
   - **Bottom Legal Bar**: Thin divider line with corporate copyright and legal links (`Privacy Policy`, `Terms of Use`).

2. **Removal of Irrelevant Public Elements**:
   - **Excised "⭐ Write a Customer Review" Button**: Removed the customer review button from the public footer, eliminating awkward consumer friction from a B2B/franchise-focused corporate web portal.
   - **Excised "Verify Certificate" Badge from Public Footer**: Removed the sample certificate verification link (`EF-2026-8842`) from the public footer. The underlying verification API and route (`/verify-certificate/[uid]`) remain functional for background verification checks via printed certificate QR codes, but are no longer cluttering the customer navigation.
   - **Excised Embedded Map Iframe**: Removed the heavy, slow-loading Google Maps iframe from the footer in favor of clean typographic address details.

3. **Complete Elimination & Permanent Redirection of `/services`**:
   - Permanently deleted the directory `frontend/app/services/` and its `page.tsx` from the codebase.
   - Configured an automatic `308 Permanent Redirect` in `next.config.js` redirecting any incoming `/services` traffic to the homepage (`/`).
   - Sanitized all legacy internal `/services` links in `revenue-streamdesk.tsx`, `what-makes-different.tsx`, and `layout-client-wrapper.tsx` to point to `/franchise` and `/about`.

4. **Header vs. Footer Navigation Harmonization**:
   - Maintained the top header navbar in its focused, uncluttered state: `Home`, `About Us`, `Franchise`, `Contact Us` + `Apply for Franchise` CTA.
   - Positioned `Careers` inside the footer navigation bar to keep the top header clean while ensuring full accessibility to live job postings.
