## 1. Executive Summary

This release delivers a complete overhaul of the **EcoFone Admin Console**, transitioning it from a dark theme into a **clean, light-mode SaaS CMS & Operations Dashboard** modeled directly after the **Setu Startup School Admin Panel design system**, styled with EcoFone's brand colors (Canvas `#f8fafc`, pure white `#ffffff` cards, borders `#e2e8f0`, soft emerald `#ecfdf5` / `#059669` active pills, and EcoFone orange `#ea580c` accents).

Simultaneously, a complete **Content Management System (CMS) engine with direct device image/file upload** was integrated across all public sections of the EcoFone website, backed by a dynamic two-way synchronization layer while **preserving 100% of existing operational data and modules**:

- **Setu-Inspired Light-Mode Design System**: Clean slate canvas (`#f8fafc`), pure white containers, 260px fixed white sidebar with 5 categorized navigation groups, emerald active navigation pills, 64px sticky top header bar with dynamic breadcrumbs, global search pill, and `View Live Page ↗` action.
- **Full Website Content Management System (CMS)**:
  - *Hero & Homepage*: Tabbed editors for Hero Banner, UP Map pin cities, Device Storefront Photo Upload, Device Brochure PDF Upload, 4-step Investment Model, 4 Unit Economics KPI cards, Multiple Revenue Streams (Buy, Sell, Repair with device upload), 2 Warranty/Trial guarantee cards, 6 "What Makes EcoFone Different" feature cards, and 3 Market Research stat cards.
  - *Research & Press*: Setu table & modal editor with device cover image upload.
  - *Store Locator (Outlets)*: Unified outlet cards manager with device storefront photo upload, timings, phone, address, and Google Maps embed.
  - *Partner Video Stories*: Video & story cards manager with device thumbnail upload, YouTube embed URL, ratings, and quotes.
  - *Testimonials & Reviews*: Dual-tab manager for Investor Testimonials and Customer Reviews with avatar upload, star ratings, quotes, initials, and status toggles.
  - *FAQs Manager*: Comprehensive FAQ manager with modal edit and Up/Down reordering.
  - *Site Settings & Footer*: Contact info, social media URLs, WhatsApp CTA number, and **Homepage Section Visibility Toggles**.
- **100% Data & Operational Module Preservation**: All 43 Franchise Leads, 2 Customer Inquiries, 12 Verification Records, 7 Outlets, Team Members, Security Settings, System Export Logs, and 28 TechByte Blogs (including the LinkedIn-style Composer) remain completely intact and functional in light mode.
- **Critical Bug Fix in Public Homepage**: Resolved the broken testimonial carousel in `split-reviews.tsx` where clicking navigation arrows (`<` or `>`) shifted entire blocks into an empty white void. Replaced with card-by-card translation (`calc(-${currentSlide * (340 + 24)}px)`) and strict clamping.
- **Two-Way Dynamic Synchronization**: Built dedicated Next.js App Router API handlers (`/api/cms/content` and `/api/cms/upload`) with thread-safe JSON persistence (`frontend/data/website_cms.json`) ensuring immediate two-way live sync between the Admin Console and the public website.

## 4. Detailed Component Engineering

### A. Hero & Homepage CMS (`hero-cms-manager.tsx`)
- Provides 5 segmented sub-tabs:
  1. **Hero Banner**: Live text controls for top badge, headline, orange highlight, description, and primary CTA. Supports direct device upload for the storefront background image and brochure PDF. UP Map pin cities can be dynamically added, edited, or removed.
  2. **Investment & Unit Economics**: Controls the 4 sequential investment steps and 4 unit economics KPI cards (`Total Investment`, `Store Format`, `Earning Structure`, `Payback Period`).
  3. **Revenue Streams & Guarantees**: Controls the 2 warranty/replacement guarantee cards and 3 primary retail channels (`Buy Used Smartphones`, `Sell Certified Refurbished`, `Repair & Accessories`) with device photo upload.
  4. **What Makes EcoFone Different**: Full CRUD on the 6 differentiator cards with icon picker and highlight colors.
  5. **Market Growth Stats**: Edit values and sources for Counterpoint, Financial Express, and Market Research Future data points.

### B. Store Locator & Outlets CMS (`stores-manager.tsx`)
- Unified physical store management with device photo upload, address textarea, operating timings, store telephone, and Google Maps embed URL with live preview.

### C. Partner Video Stories CMS (`partner-stories-manager.tsx`)
- Grid and modal manager for video storytelling cards featuring direct device thumbnail uploads, YouTube video embed URLs, location badges, star ratings, and partner statements.

### D. Testimonials & Reviews CMS (`testimonials-manager.tsx`)
- Segmented dual-tab manager for `Investor Testimonials` ("Listen What Our Investors Say") and `Customer Reviews` ("Listen What Our Customers Say") supporting star ratings, avatars, quotes, initials, and active/hidden toggles.

### E. FAQs Manager (`faqs-manager.tsx`)
- List view of all accordion items with `+ Add New FAQ`, inline edit, delete, and Up/Down reordering.

### F. Site Settings & Section Visibility (`site-settings-manager.tsx`)
- **Card 1: Contact & Social Settings**: Registered address, business email, contact phone, WhatsApp CTA number, Google Maps embed URL, and social media handles.
- **Card 2: Section Visibility Controls**: 10 instant toggle switches enabling/disabling individual homepage sections dynamically without redeployment.

### G. Testimonial Carousel Sliding Bug Fix (`split-reviews.tsx`)
- **Bug**: On the public homepage, clicking `<` or `>` shifted slides using full-width percentage jumps (`currentSlide * 100%`), moving beyond the available 3-card view into a blank white screen.
- **Fix**: Re-implemented sliding with precise card-width translation:
  ```tsx
  style={{ transform: `translateX(calc(-${currentSlide * (340 + 24)}px))` }}
  ```
  with strict index clamping:
  ```tsx
  const maxSlide = Math.max(0, items.length - 3);
  const nextSlide = () => setCurrentSlide((prev) => (prev >= maxSlide ? 0 : prev + 1));
  const prevSlide = () => setCurrentSlide((prev) => (prev <= 0 ? maxSlide : prev - 1));
  ```
