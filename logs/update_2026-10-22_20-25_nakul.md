## 1. Executive Summary

This engineering sprint addressed two major initiatives: a complete enterprise-grade refactoring and brand harmonization of the **EcoFone Franchise Page (`/franchise`)**, and the resolution of the broken navigation **404 error on `/services`** through the creation of a dedicated, high-trust **Our Services** portal.

Simultaneously, all "vibe-coded" template artifacts (excessive pill buttons, pastel circular icon backdrops, raw unpopulated template variables, and layout collision bugs) were completely eliminated, aligning the page geometry, typography, and color palette directly with EcoFone's signature **Home (`/`)** and **About Us (`/about`)** design language:

- **Complete Elimination of "Vibe-Coded" Artifacts**:
  - Removed all `rounded-full`, `rounded-3xl`, `rounded-2xl`, and raw `rounded-none` brutalist containers across cards, badges, and form inputs. Standardized to refined **`rounded-xl` (12px)** cards, **`rounded-lg` (8px)** buttons and form inputs, and **`rounded-md` (6px)** category badges.
  - Eradicated 100% of unicode emojis and corrupted character encodings (`âœ“`, `😀"„`, `😀"¦`, `😀š€`), replacing them with crisp, 1.5px stroke `lucide-react` vector icons housed in soft-tinted square containers (`bg-orange-50` and `bg-[#eefcf2]`).
  - Resolved all unpopulated template variables: `[INVESTMENT RANGE]` &rarr; `₹10 Lakh to ₹25 Lakh`, `[MARGIN %]` &rarr; `15–25% margins`, and `[PAYBACK PERIOD]` &rarr; `12–24 months payback`.
- **Urgent Layout Collision & Scroll Offset Fixes**:
  - **FICO vs. FIFO Center Connector**: Resolved the desktop collision bug where the absolutely-positioned EcoFone connector badge and `"Operate Hands-On"` label overlapped `"CORE STRUCTURAL ADVANTAGES"` inside the right FIFO card. Re-engineered the layout into a clean **3-column desktop grid (`grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-[1fr_auto_1fr] gap-6 lg:gap-8 items-stretch`)** with a dedicated center bridge column.
  - **Sticky Navbar Clearance**: Added `scroll-mt-28` (`scroll-margin-top: 7rem`) to all section IDs (`#fico-breakdown`, `#partner-breakdown`, `#deployment-roadmap`, `#apply-franchise-form`, `#choose-model`) and generous vertical padding (`py-20 lg:py-24`), preventing headings, watermark numerals, and form fields from being clipped under the 80px sticky header.
- **Progressive Disclosure & Interactive Elements**:
  - Replaced wall-to-wall data table clutter with clean, 3-column executive summary metric strips (`₹20–25 Lakh`, `20–24 Mos. Payback`, `3% or 1.5% Return Floor`) and interactive line-item disclosure toggles (`"View Line-Item Capital Allocation [+]"` / `"Hide Breakdown [-]"`).
  - Wired the top model cards (`"View Detailed Breakdown ->"`) to smoothly scroll and automatically expand the relevant breakdown via custom events.
  - Built an interactive client-side FAQ accordion (`franchise-faq-accordion.tsx`) with left-border brand accents (`border-l-4 border-[#E85D04]`) and animated chevron indicators.
- **Enterprise Form Implementation (`#apply-franchise-form`)**:
  - Re-implemented the franchise lead generation form with controlled state, field-level error handling in `#DC2626`, strict 10-digit Indian mobile regex validation (`/^[6-9]\d{9}$/`), email validation, custom dropdown selects, and double-click prevention with loading spinner.
- **Resolution of `/services` 404 Route**:
  - Built a dedicated, high-converting **Our Services (`/services`)** portal featuring the 3 core re-commerce pillars: **Buy Used Smartphones (`#buy`)**, **Sell Certified Refurbished (`#sell`)**, and **Express Repairs & Accessories (`#repair`)**, backed by official guarantees (32-point inspection, 6-month warranty, 3-day trial exchange, and 100% deep data sanitization).

## 3. Detailed Component Engineering

### A. Dedicated 3-Column Comparison Grid (`franchise-detailed-models.tsx`)
- **Bug Fixed**: Previously, the center connector graphic was absolutely positioned with negative offsets (`right-[-75px]`), causing it to collide with text in the right FIFO card on screens between 1024px and 1280px.
- **Architectural Solution**: Replaced with an explicit 3-column grid:
  ```tsx
  <div className="grid grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-[1fr_auto_1fr] gap-6 lg:gap-8 items-stretch max-w-6xl mx-auto">
    {/* Column 1: FICO Card */}
    ...
    {/* Column 2: Dedicated Center Connector (No overlap possible) */}
    <div className="hidden lg:flex flex-col items-center justify-center px-2 py-4 self-center space-y-4 min-w-[150px]">
      ...
    </div>
    {/* Column 3: FIFO Card */}
    ...
  </div>
  ```

### B. Deployment Roadmap with Watermark Numerals (`#deployment-roadmap`)
- Modernized the 4-step deployment process (`01. Agreement & Legal`, `02. Store Setup & Fitout`, `03. Stock & Staff Training`, `04. Launch & Operations`) into `rounded-xl bg-[#FAF9F6]` cards with large, subtle watermark numbers (`font-display font-black text-5xl text-slate-200/60 group-hover:text-orange-200/70 transition-colors`).
- Soft-tinted square icon containers (`w-11 h-11 rounded-xl bg-orange-50` and `bg-[#eefcf2]`).

### C. Progressive Disclosure & Collapsible Capital Allocation
- High-level executive benchmark bars show key metrics (`₹20–25 Lakh`, `20–24 Mos. Payback`, `3% or 1.5% Return`) in refined cards.
- Interactive toggle buttons allow prospective franchisees to reveal or hide the full line-item capital allocation table.
- Table rows feature smooth micro-interactions with left accent bars in Brand Orange (`#E85D04`).

### D. Creation of `/services` Route (`frontend/app/services/page.tsx`)
- **Problem**: Navbar link, footer links, and homepage revenue desk routed users to `/services`, but Next.js returned a 404 error because the page did not exist.
- **Solution**: Implemented a comprehensive, SEO-optimized services portal with Next.js Metadata, structured open-graph tags, and 3 anchor-targeted service pillars:
  - **`#buy`**: Instant Cash Buyback Service (fair valuation algorithm, 100% deep data sanitization, instant UPI payout).
  - **`#sell`**: Certified Refurbished Flagships up to 70% off (32-point inspection, 6-month warranty, 3-day trial replacement).
  - **`#repair`**: Express Service Lab & Accessories (30-minute screen/battery replacement, 90-day parts warranty, GaN chargers).
  - **Assurance & Franchise CTAs**: Official guarantee strip and partner expansion banner.

### E. Enterprise Lead Generation Form (`franchise-lead-form.tsx`)
- Form inputs, select dropdowns, and buttons updated to refined `rounded-lg` (8px).
- Strict validation rules:
  - Full Name: Minimum 2 characters.
  - Mobile: Strict Indian 10-digit mobile validation (`/^[6-9]\d{9}$/`).
  - Email: Standard RFC email format validation.
  - City & State: Required text inputs.
  - Budget & Model: Custom select dropdowns with chevron icons.
  - Commercial Space Ownership: Custom radio options (`Yes` / `No`).
- Async submission with loading spinner (`Loader2`) and disabled submit button to prevent double-clicks.
- Rectangular green success alert banner upon successful submission.
