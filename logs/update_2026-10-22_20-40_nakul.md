## 1. Executive Summary

This update fulfills the design restoration request for the **Choose Franchise Model (FICO vs FIFO)** comparison section on the **EcoFone Franchise Page (`/franchise`)**, bringing back the original approved design layout with the central EcoFone circular badge, curved flow arrows, and distinct high-converting card aesthetics:

- **Restoration of Original Model Comparison Architecture (`ChooseFranchiseModel`)**:
  - Reinstated the centered clean header:
    - Title: `"Choose Your Franchise Model"`
    - Subtitle: `"Flexible opportunities designed for different types of partners — whether you want to invest passively or run the store hands-on."`
  - **FICO Card (Left)**:
    - Restored the warm peach container background (`bg-[#fff9f5]`) with `rounded-3xl` border geometry, soft orange shadow (`shadow-[0_20px_40px_-15px_rgba(234,88,12,0.1)]`), and `border-2 border-orange-100`.
    - Restored the top-right orange squircle icon badge (`w-16 h-16 bg-orange-100 rounded-2xl flex items-center justify-center text-ecoOrange-500`) housing the briefcase icon.
    - Restored the `FICO` pill badge (`bg-orange-100 text-ecoOrange-600 font-black px-4 py-1.5 rounded-full text-sm`).
    - Maintained the 4 value points with circular orange checkmark badges (`w-5 h-5 rounded-full bg-ecoOrange-500 text-white`).
    - Restored the full-width orange pill CTA button (`bg-[#ea580c] hover:bg-[#c2410c] text-white font-bold py-4 rounded-full transition-colors shadow-lg`) with active `onClick={scrollToFico}` smooth scroll.
  - **FIFO Card (Right)**:
    - Restored the light mint container background (`bg-[#f5fbf7]`) with `rounded-3xl` border geometry, soft emerald shadow (`shadow-[0_20px_40px_-15px_rgba(16,185,129,0.1)]`), and `border-2 border-emerald-100`.
    - Restored the top-right green squircle icon badge (`w-16 h-16 bg-emerald-100 rounded-2xl flex items-center justify-center text-emerald-600`) housing the store/home icon.
    - Restored the `FIFO` pill badge (`bg-emerald-200 text-emerald-800 font-black px-4 py-1.5 rounded-full text-sm`).
    - Maintained the 4 value points with circular emerald checkmark badges (`w-5 h-5 rounded-full bg-emerald-600 text-white`).
    - Restored the full-width dark green pill CTA button (`bg-[#061C0F] hover:bg-emerald-900 text-white font-bold py-4 rounded-full transition-colors shadow-lg`) with active `onClick={scrollToShopInShop}` smooth scroll.
  - **Center Connector Graphic (Desktop)**:
    - Centered circular white brand badge (`w-32 h-32 bg-white rounded-full shadow-xl border border-slate-100`) displaying the official EcoFone logo (`/logo.jpg`).
    - Dual curved directional SVG flow arrows:
      - Left orange curved arrow with italic label `"Invest Passively"` pointing to FICO.
      - Right emerald curved arrow with italic label `"Operate Hands-On"` pointing to FIFO.
    - Set `pointer-events-none` on container and `pointer-events-auto` on clickable child elements to ensure full interaction fidelity without blocking card interaction.
